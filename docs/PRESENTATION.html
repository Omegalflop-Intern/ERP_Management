Accounting

Double-Entry Financial Accounting & Statements

Real-time general ledger, automated debit/credit balancing, trial balance, P&L statements, and investor dividend tracking.

Financial Statements Matrix
  • Balance Sheet: Assets = Liabilities + Equity (Real-time sync).
  • Profit & Loss: Revenue - COGS - Expenses = Net Profit.
  • Chart of Accounts: Customizable 5-tier financial account hierarchy.
  • Journal Entries: Manual multi-line vouchers with validation.
Investor & Asset Management
  • Investor Portfolios: Capital pool tracking & profit distribution share calculation.
  • Fixed Assets: Straight-line asset depreciation schedules.
  • Business Loans: Loan principal & EMI interest calculation.

Security

Granular control over 26 enterprise modules with role permissions matrix, JWT bearer security, and automatic brute-force logging.
